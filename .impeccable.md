## Design Context

### Users
Holiday makers and families planning self-catering accommodation in Umhlanga Rocks, KwaZulu-Natal. Browsing on mobile and desktop, often comparing options. Need to quickly understand what's available, trust the operator, and make contact or enquire.

### Brand Personality
Established, personal, coastal. Three words: **warm, trusted, coastal**.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-first. Clean whites and the brand's teal/navy palette. No cream or warm-sand tones — these are off-palette. The brand logo uses teal (`#29B8D8`), navy (`#1A2B5E`), and red (`#D42B2B`). Sections alternate between white/light-teal-tint and navy to create rhythm. Dark sections use white text; light sections use navy/charcoal text with teal accents.

### Design Principles
1. **Brand-true colour only** — teal, navy, white. No cream, sand, or warm neutrals.
2. **Light/dark rhythm** — sections alternate to create visual separation without borders or dividers.
3. **Coastal but refined** — avoid beach-kitsch; think clean, trusted, premium self-catering.
4. **Typography-led** — Gloock (display serif) paired with Jost (body sans). Let the type do the work.
5. **Personal over corporate** — the 37-year family history is a key differentiator; design should feel human, not slick.
